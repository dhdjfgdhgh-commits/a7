About Anoraks

We write for people who go out in the rain anyway.

Anoraks is an independent site about rain jackets and wet-weather layering.

We explain technical labels in plain language, show how to choose a jacket for your weather and activity, and share care routines that keep a shell working for years. We do not sell jackets or receive payment for recommendations.
